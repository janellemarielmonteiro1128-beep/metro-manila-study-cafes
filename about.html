About the Guide

Finding a comfortable spot with good coffee, reliable Wi-Fi, and power outlets can be tough. This guide was created to highlight top spots for students and remote workers across Metro Manila looking for productive work environments.

Every recommendation in this guide comes from personal experience visiting coffee shops to find spaces with great lighting, accessible outlets, peaceful ambiance, and excellent espresso choices.

About Me

Hi! I'm Janelle, a student creator who spends plenty of time studying in coffee shops. I built this guide to help fellow students find reliable, cozy, and aesthetic study spots around the metro.
