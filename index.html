Metro Manila Study Cafes

A curated guide to cozy, quiet, and study-friendly coffee shops around Metro Manila.

About the Guide

Finding a comfortable spot with good coffee, reliable Wi-Fi, and power outlets can be tough. This guide highlights top spots for students and remote workers.

My Top Picks

Warehouse Coffee

Sampaloc, Manila

Spacious study haven with great seating, glowing neon signage, and a dedicated workspace setup for long study sessions.

Treehouse Poblacion

Poblacion, Makati

A unique, atmospheric spot with lush greenery and warm lighting, perfect for a cozy late-night study or coffee hangout.

Majikasa Cafe

Metro Manila

A sleek, industrial-style cafe counter featuring professional espresso gear, specialty brews, and a cozy aesthetic.
